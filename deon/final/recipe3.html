Ingredients for brown butter sauce

  • 11 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 3 clovs garlic thinly sliced
  • 1/2 teaspoon pepper
  • 1 cup grated parmesan
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice

Directions

  • In a skillet melt the butter and cheese over medium heat. Cook, swirling occasionally, until the foam subsides, the milk solids turn golden-brown and it smells nutty and toasty, 3 to 4 minutes. Remove from heat immediately.

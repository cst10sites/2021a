Ingredients for tomato sauce

  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic,thinly sliced
  • pinch of salt
  • pinch of pepper
  • One 28-ounce can of tomatoes, crushed
  • 1 cup torn basil leaves
  • 12 ounces spaghetti
  • Grated Parmesan, optional
  • Bring a large pot of water to a boil.

Directions

  • Cook the oil, garlic, 1/2 teaspoon salt and pepper in a large skillet over medium heat, stirring, until the oil heats up and the garlic just starts to turn golden, 4 to 6 minutes.
  • Add the tomatoes; rinse the can with about 1 cup of water, and add the water to the skillet.
  • Add the basil, raise the heat to medium-high and simmer until the sauce thickens, 15 to 20 minutes.

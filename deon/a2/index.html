How to make a peanut butter and jelly sandwich.

If you're allergic to peanut butter go away

Introduction

The first peanut butter and jelly sandwich recipe documented was the Boston Cooking School Magazine of Culinary Science and Domestic Economics written by Julia Davis Chandler. She said to use currant or crab-apple jelly and called the combination delicious. Since then the PB and J sandwich has been one of the most famous, easy to make and delictable lunch items.


Ingredients (one serving):

  • 2 slices of white bread
  • 1.2 teaspoons of peanut butter
  • 1.7 teaspoons of jam (strawberry prefered)

Instructions

  1. Take a knife and spread the peanut butter on one of the slices of bread.
  2. Take a knife and spread the jam on one of the slices of bread.
  3. Take the peanut butter covered side of one of the slices and put it together with the jam covered side of the other.

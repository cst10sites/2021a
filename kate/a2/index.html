OMELETTE RECIPE

An omelette recipe! It is a very very epic recipe that everyone should try:). You can probably make a filling for it if you wanted, but I'll just put the instructions for the egg part. I haven't tried it, but the reviews say its good, so... Enjoy!

Ingredients

  • 1tbsp of butter
  • 3 eggs
  • 2tbsp cream or 1½tbsp of milk
  • salt & pepper (to taste)
  • 1⅓ cup of grated cheese

Instructions

  1. Whisk together eggs, cream/milk, and salt & pepper
  2. Put skillet at medium heat
  3. Add butter, when it starts to foam, add in the eggs. Give it a few stirs when you put it in the skillet, but once the base starts to set spread it around the skillet.
  4. Do this "Wait 20 - 30 seconds, then use a rubber spatula to lift up the edge, tilt pan to make the egg run off the surface under the omelette. Repeat 2 or 3 times around the edges of the omelette."
  5. Sprinkle half of the omelette with cheese and whatever else you want on it
  6. Cover with lid and let it cook for 30 seconds or until the under side is light golden.
  7. Fold the omelette in half and transfer it to a plate

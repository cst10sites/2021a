Facts and stuff

  • Claude Monet was born on the 14th of November, 1840 and died December 5th, 1926
  • He was a French painter, born in Paris, but moved to Giverny
  • In his early twenties, Monet was drafted into the military
  • He was a founder of French Impressionist painting
  • Monet's painting "Impression, Sunrise" was actually how the term "impressionism" got it's name
  • Monet would paint the same landscape several times in order to capure it in different seasons, and lights (2 paintings at the top of the water lily pond)
  • Monet died of lung cancer at the age of 86

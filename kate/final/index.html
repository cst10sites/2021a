Famous Artists


Intro

Painting. It was one of the first inventions. Traces of painting have been found in caves from over 40,000 years ago. Before, colourful rocks, bones,and minerals were ground into powder in order to make a pigment. Then mixed with egg, or animal byproducts to bind them together to get paint. Now however, the pigments are mostly synthetic.

There are many differnt types of paints, but the three main ones are oil, acrylics, and watercolours. Many famous artists such as Leonardo da Vinci, Claude Monet and Vincent van Gogh used oil paints for their paintings.

How to make Kraft Dinner

Hello! My name is Frank Reid, and on this page I will give step by step instructions on how to make Kraft Dinner. Despite this food being quite simple to make, I found many who have had trouble in the past while doing so. I chose this food because 1, it is very simple and quick to make, and 2, it tastes pretty good!

Ingredients:

  • 1 box of KD
  • 6 cups of water
  • 1 tbsp of butter or margarine
  • ⅓ cup of skim milk

Instructions

  1. Boil 6 cups of water. Add the pasta, then cook for 7 to 8 minutes. Make sure to occasionally stir.
  2. Drain, then return to saucepan.
  3. Add 1 tbsp of butter or margarine, ⅓ cup of skim milk and the Cheese Sauce Mix (which comes with the package)
  4. Mix well.
  5. You're done!

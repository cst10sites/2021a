Italian Cheese Herbed Foccacia

serves 2


Introduction

This is an italian flatbread with herbs and cheese. This flatbread is mostly used as an appetizer for italian resturants. This is a very good recipe because, you can add whatever type of herb you want, so each one is very unique every time.

Ingredients:

  • 165 mL flour
  • 5 mL instant yeast
  • 1 mL salt
  • 1 mL sugar
  • 1 mL garlic powder
  • 3 mL of random dried herbs
  • A pinch of ground pepper
  • 5 mL vegetable oil
  • 5 mL olive oil
  • 60 mL warm water
  • 2 more mL of random dried herbs
  • Some cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 450 °F. Grease a loaf pan with oil.
  2. In a medium bowl, sift flour. Add yeast, salt, sugar, garlic powder, pepper, and the 3 mL of random herbs.
  3. Combine vegetable oil and warm water in a liquid mesure. pour liquid into dry ingredients and stir mixture until dough starts to come together. (Add more water if it's too dry)
  4. Form dough into a ball and turn out onto a lightly floured surface. Knead dough until smooth and elastic.
  5. Lightly oil a small bowl, place dough in bowl and turn to coat with oil. Cover with a damp cloth and let rise in a warm place for 25 minutes.
  6. Punch dow dough and place into greased pan. With fingertips, press dough into orners and side of pan.
  7. Brush with olive oil, sprinkle top with cheese and random dried herbs.
  8. Bake for 15-20 minutes or until golden brown and cheese is bubbly.
  9. Serve warm

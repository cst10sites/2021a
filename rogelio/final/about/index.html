What is the Fibonacci sequence?

The Fibonacci sequence is a sequence of numbers that is calculated by adding one number in the sequence by the number before it in the sequence. The sequence starts at zero and one. The next number is calculated by adding zero and one, so it would be one. The sequence then continues in the same manner. The Fibonacci sequence is also used to calculate the golden ratio. The golden ratio is calculated by dividing one number in the sequence by the number before it in the sequence. The golden ratio calculated more accuratly when using higher numbers in the sequence. The golden ratio is most commonly used as 1.618. A rectangle with the side lengths of one and the golden ratio. This type of rectangle can be split into a perfect square and create a scaled down version of the previous rectangle.

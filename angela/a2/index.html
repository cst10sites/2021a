How to Make Kale Chips©


Kale chips are one of my favourite healthy snacks. They are very similar to chips because they are very crispy and have a very nice flavour. Today you will learn how to make the most basic type of kale chips with ingredients you probably have at home already.

Ingredients!

  • 1 head of kale
  • 30ml olive oil
  • salt
  • pepper
  • 15ml chilli flakes

Steps to making kale chips:

  1. Wash your head of kale thoroughly
  2. Pre-heat you air fryer to 180°C
  3. Break the head of kale into small peices
  4. Coat each peice of kale with olive oil
  5. Add the chilli flakes to the kale and massage it in
  6. Put as much salt and pepper as you like
  7. Spread a thin layer of kale on your air fryer basket
  8. let it fry for 5-6 minutes and shake the basket every minute or two
  9. Do the last 2 steps for the rest of the kale
  10. Enjoy!!

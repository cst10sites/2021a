Childhood

Ariana Grande was born in Boco Raton, Florida on June 26th, 1993 to her mother, Joan Grande and father, Ed Butera. Growing up Ariana was a bit of a weird kid. She loved watching horror movies from a young age and collected many Halloween masks. Besides that, Ariana also loved singing and acting, so much that she stared in many plays in her community like "Annie". She also sang the American national anthem for a stadium full of people when she was only eight years old. In 2008, Ariana Landed her biggest role of Charlotte in the Broadway production "13" when she was 15. While working on the musical she made a good friend, LIz Gillies whom she is still close with today. Her love for musical theatre is also thanks to her big brother Frankie Grande who she is still is very close to.

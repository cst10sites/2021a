Ariana grande

Ariana Grande-Butera, a 27 year old American-singer is one of the most famous popstars in the World, with her most popular songs having over one billion streams. She has also won many awards like the Nickelodeon Kids' Choice Award, Billboard Music Award, Women of the Year and many more. Besides being one of the most influencial figures in the music industry, she also has a huge social media presence with 205 million followers on instagram, 77.4 million on twitter, 44.3 million subscribers on youtube and 52.41 million on spotify. On top of all of her fame she still remains humble and hardworking seeing how she has been heavily involved in the making of all six of her studio albums. She is an inspriation to many because of her talent, kind heart, limited amounts of scandels and ability to stay strong especially after everything that has happened to her in these past years.

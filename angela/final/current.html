Current/Positions Era

Ariana has been working very hard even after her big hit of an album, Thank u next was released around two years ago. Although Grande said she would be taking a break after her last album, that didn't last for long. After multiple snippits being released throughout the year and a hit single("Stuck with u") and collab with Lady Gaga("Rain on me"), Arianators practically knew AG6 was coming soon. Finally on October 14th, 2020, the queen announced that she will be dropping her album that month. Thankfully she released the lead single shortly before her album release so that her hungry fans had something to munch on. The wait was long but it was worth it, on October 30th all her fans from around the world waited for its release. Positions consisted of 14 songs each carefully made with love and passion. Very quickly all 14 tracks made it onto the Billboard Hot 100 and broke an astounding record which was previously held by Miley Cyrus. Several Days after the funky, R&B, poppy, jazzy album was relesed, Ariana did an interview with Zach Sang to talk about her album. This album was her time to move on and close the mournful Thank u next era and do something a bit more light-hearted. Even though most of the tracks seem to lack any depth, there are a few messages only Arianators can spot. For example, in the last song, "POV", there is a line that sounded familiar. The line "All my baggage fadin' safelty" is definatley related to the line in her song Ghostin "...Im a girl with a whole lot of baggage". This is so sad and sweet because Ghostin was about her ex, Mac Miller's over dose and her marriage falling apart but it is nice to see that she is finally moving on. During interview Ariana also mentioned how she won't see herself touring anytime soon with the state of the world and thinks she might do a double album tour like how she did for the Sweetener tour. During this quarantine Ari has also released a new fragrance called R.E.M. which is inspired by and named after her song. However, just recently MS.Grande posted a new comercial for a pt. 2 of the frangrance. If this year wasn't saved by Ariana already there is a Sweetner World tour movie that should be coming out soon and AG is also expected to be featured in an upcoming film. Nonetheless this was a big leap for Ariana Grande and for her followers too but it's just one of many more.

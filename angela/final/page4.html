  Thank u next:
  
 -Ariana's 5th studio album
 
 -Consists of 12 tracks
 
 -Released Febuary 8th, 2019
 
 -Was released 6 months after her 4th album

 -AG5 is mostly a pop album but has some R&B and trap elements

 -This album had a heavy emphasis on her past relationships and was very emotional
 
 -Grande went on tour for this album and her last album from March 18th, 2019 to December 22nd, 2019

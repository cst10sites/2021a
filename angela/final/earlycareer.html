Early Career

Ariana Grande really started to rise to fame from 2010 to 2013, after getting casted as the role of Cat Valentine for a the show "Victorious". Her character was one liked by many. Cat was a funny, bubbly and cute character. Unfortunatly during her time performing in the sitcom, Ms.Grande was forced to dye her hair red very frequently, which ultimatley destroyed her hair. The only way to cover up the damge was to get hair extesions and to keep her hair in a half-up half-down hairdo or a high ponytail, both of which have become her signature hairstyles. She also was in the show "Sam and Cat" where it dweveled a little bit deeeper into Cats life and featured another famous character from "Icarly", called Sam. While shooting on the set of Victorious, Ariana also signed a contract with Republic records and soon put out her first single called "Put Your Hearts Up", thus marking the beggining of her music career.

Early Albums

Yours Truly:
																								
- First studio album ever created
																
- Recorded over a three year period	
																	
- Was released on August 30th, 2013	
																	
- Has 12 songs in total

- Had many major hits like "The Way" ft.Mac Miller, 
"Baby I" and "Right There" ft.Big Sean

- The album explored a more retro style and R&B sound

- Yours Truly reached top-ten positions in the album
charts of Australia, Canada, Denmark, Ireland, Japan,
the Netherlands and the United Kingdom!

- Ariana co-wrote 6/12 of the songs

	
  My Everything:
  
- Second studio album

- Released on August 25th, 2014

- Consists of 12 songs

- Had many major hits like "Problem" ft. Iggy Azalea, 
"One Last Time" and "Break Free"

- Similar to Yours Truly, this album  also has a
 pop-R&B style
 
- My Everything was so popular that it was nominated
 for Best Pop Vocal Album
 
- Ariana embarked on her first world tour called 
the Honeymoon Tour to perform this album

- Grande co-wrote 4/12 of the songs on the album

  
  Dangerous Women:
  
- Third studio album

- Released on May 20th, 2016

- Album consists of 15 songs

- Major hits include: "Dangerous Women", "Into You" 
and "Side to Side" ft. Nicki Minaj

- Dangerous Women has a sultry, pop, R&B sound to it

- The Dangerous Women album also receiveved a Grammy
nomination for Best Pop Album

-The Dangerous Women tour started on Feburary 3rd,
2017 and ended September 21st, 2017

- The tour was interupted by an unfortunate 
suicidal bombing attack in the Manchster 
Arena that killed 23

- Shortly after the the attack, Grande hosted a 
concert called "One Love Manchester" to help 
victims and their families

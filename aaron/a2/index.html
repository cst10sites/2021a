How to Make Stroopwafels

What are stroopwafels?

A stroopwafel is a wafer cookie made from two thin layers of baked dough joined by a caramel filling.
Stroopwafels were first made in the Netherlands in 1904 and they are best served with a cup of tea or coffee.
With this recipe, you will learn to make these delicious treats.

Prep: 45 mins
Cook: 1 min
Additional: 45 mins
Total: 1 hr 31 mins
Servings: 12
Yield: 1 dozen

Ingredients

Waffle

  • 4 cups of all purpose flour
  • 1 cup of butter, melted
  • ¾ cupswhite sugar
  • ¼ cup of lukewarm milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2(.25 ounce) packages active dry yeast

Filling

  • 1 cup of molasses or black treacle
  • 1⅓ cups of soft brown sugar
  • ¼ cup butter
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon

Directions

  1. Combine flour, 1 cup melted butter, white sugar, milk, egg, and yeast in a large bowl.
    Knead until dough is smooth and elastic. Cover loosely with plastic wrap and let rise,
    about 45 minutes.
  2. Heat treacle, brown sugar, ¼ cup butter, and cinnamon in a small saucepan over
    medium heat until melted together, 3 to 5 minutes. Mix well until filling is smooth.
  3. Preheat a shallow waffle iron according to manufacturer's instructions.
  4. Knead dough briefly and divide it into balls about 1½ inches in diameter, or as
    needed to fit your waffle iron.
  5. Bake 1 ball of dough the preheated iron until golden brown and iron stops steaming,
    30 seconds to 1 minute. Remove with a cake spatula. Press down with a round cutter
    to trim edges for a perfect circle. Carefully slice horizontally into 2 rounds while still
    hot. Spread 1 to 2 tablespoons of the filling on each round and sandwich together.
    Repeat with remaining dough.

Cook's Note

Making stroopwafels does require a special shallow waffle iron, as regular waffle irons
will produce waffles too thick for this purpose. Try finding a stroopwafel iron, if
possible, or improvise with either a pizzelle iron or waffle ice cream cone iron.

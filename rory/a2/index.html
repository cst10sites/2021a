The Beef Wellington

The beef wellington is a dish with many deep historical roots in europian culture. It is said that the wellington got its name from the Duke of Wellington, in the early nineteenth century;when he was revolting against the force of napolean. The other not so flashy story is that it was frist served at a dinner-party in the new zealand capital of Wellington, in the mid-nineteenth century. The delicacy consists of a cut of beef called the tenderloin, that is then wrapped in a decadent mushroom layer called duxelle, then it has a meat, and finally a pastry outside.


Ingredients Needed:

General:

  • 650g of Tenderloin
  • 24ml of Canola Oil
  • 1 pinch of Salt
  • 1 pinch of Pepper
  • 24ml of Dijon Mustard
  • 1.5 sheets of Puff Pastry
  • 6 slices of Prosciutto Salami

Duxelle:

  • 460g of Mushrooms
  • 2 shallots
  • 3 cloves of Garlic
  • 24ml of Olive Oil
  • 2 sprigs of Thyme
  • 1 pinch of Salt
  • 1 pinch of Pepper

Crepes:

  • 50g of All-Purpose Flour
  • 140ml of Milk
  • 1 Egg
  • 1 pinch of Salt

Egg Wash:

  • 2 Eggs
  • 15ml of Water

Instructions:

  1. Gather all ingreadients necessary; preheat your oven to 215°C, and let the tenderloin rest at room temperature.
  2. Finely chop all your mushrooms, shallots, and garlic using a knife, and add to your pan along with olive oil, as well as salt and pepper.
  3. Cook your vegetables on medium heat, until the have simmered off most of their liquid; Then add the sprigs of thyme, and let it get absorbed into the mixture, finally remove from the heat and tranfer it into a ramikan or a bowl.
  4. Once you have let the tenderloin rest, season lots with salt and pepper, be sure to coat all sides. Sear the cut in 45ml of canola oil, on medium-high heat, only sear each side for 1.5 minutes; remember to coat your tenderloin in dijon mustard immediately after removing it from the pan.
  5. To make the crepes, you must combine the egg, milk, and flour into a large bowl and wisk together, then add a pinch of salt.
  6. Preheat a nonstick, medium-sized pan on medium heat, and add butter to the bottom; Pour in enough batter to coat the bottom of the pan, let the crepes cook for 1 minute on one side, then flip and cook a further 30 seconds, repeat the process 3 times.
  7. Line up your crepes so that they slightly overlap, and place 6-8 slices of prosciutto at the base of the line, and finally spread your duxelle over in an even layer; Lastly you want to place your tenderloin at the base, and start rolling up, creating a burrito shape, then wrap in plastic foil, and tightening it by spining the edges, once completed place it in you fridge for an hour.
  8. Roll out a sheet of puff pastery to ⅓ cm thick place the crepe burrito at the bottom, roll until the edges meet, cut off the excess, and totally encase it by pinching it shut.
  9. Brush your whole wellington in an egg wash consisting of just eggs and water, apply it generously, finally add a little bit of coarse salt over it.
  10. Now place your beef wellington in your oven, baking it on 220°C until golden brown.
  11. Lastly, let the wellington rest for 10 minutes after being taken out of the oven, then cut the wellington 2 centimeter thick slice, and enjoy!

Globes:

The first globe was created in by the ancient Greeks, after they learned that the earth was round, they discovered this when Aristole looked a the moon and saw a round shadow of earth. An important addition to the creation of globes was made Claudius Ptolemaeus, an Alexandrian astronomer and geographer in 200 A.D. He cretaed a process to fix the position of different points on the globe, he did this by using spherical coordinates, also refered to as the longitude and latitude. This paved the way for all other globes to follow, making everything teh same, as found on the globe, this led to him being refered to as The Father of Goegraphy. Recent globes being created are more targetted at children, which is sad, because they have been focussed on adults, and cartographers, for so long.

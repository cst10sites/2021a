Brief History of Cartography

The history of cartography has been filled with many differen types of maps, as there was always new areas of our planet being discovered; but one thing has stayed constant throughout this time, and this is the fact that they have interested generations for thousands of years. The first recorded map was the Babylonian World Map, made by the Babylonian empire, Circa 600 B.C. It is carved into a small clay tablet that is 45cm tall, the photo on the right side, and describes the known world at the time, which is thte area the empire occupied. Another Notable Early map, is the Da Ming Hun Yi Tu, that was made in China During the Ming Empire; The map was drawn on silk in the year 1269, and streched all the way from the Pacific, to the Atlantic along the eurasian continent, it was also one of the first topographical maps, as it included mountain ranges, like the himalaya's and the Appalachian's. This was also the First Widely distributed map, as it was popular throughout Asia and Eastern Europe.

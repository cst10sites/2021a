My recipe is a simple recipe for kraft dinner pasta. I have it for lunch sometimes, and it is quite nice and quick to make. I like it because anyone can cook it, and it's still good.

Ingredients

  • pasta
  • milk
  • butter
  • cheese powder

Instructions

  1. Bring water to boil at 100℃ in medium saucepan. Add macaroni: cook 7 to 8 min, or until tender, sturring occasionally.
  2. Drain. Return macaroni to pan.
  3. Add butter/margarine, milk, and cheese sauce. Mix well.
  4. Enjoy!

Welcome to HTML Games!

The player controls a snake bordered field. As it moves it leaves a trail behind, which looks like a moving snake. The end of the trail is in a fixed position, so the snake gets longer as it moves. The player loses when the snake runs into the screen border, a trail or other obstacle, or itself.

Flappy Bird

Flappy Bird is an arcade game in which the player controls the bird which moves persistently to the right. The player has to get the bird through gaps in walls at random heights. The bird automatically descends and only ascends when the player taps the touchscreen, or in this case, the jump button. The version here is watered down and simple, using HTML.

Try it yourself!

You can try out the simple flappy bird game for yourself, see how high of a score you can get! Good luck!
